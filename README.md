## My Idea

For this project, I wanted to make something impressive, but also useful, which both shows off my programming skills, and will help someone. To accomplish this, I turned to the engineering process:

1. **Define**
   - Define the problem - what exactly are you trying to solve?
2. **Research**
   - Research the problem, finding previous solutions, or work from other people on the same, or a similar problem.
3. **Brainstorm**
   - Come up with possible solutions, and a lot of ideas - no idea is bad at this stage. Then, sort through the ideas, choosing based on feasibility, how well it solves the problem, and then accessibility to you - eliminating solutions too expensive, or with components which you can't access.
4. **Prototype**
   - Start designing and prototyping your idea, coming up with quick and rough, imperfect versions of your solution. Create prototypes with a specific aspect to test, so can perfect one aspect at a time
5. **Test**
   - Test your prototypes, and record results. If something works, add it to your final version, and polish it. If it doesn't, go back to **Prototype**, and try another version.
6. **Final Version**
   - Compile all of the working versions from your prototyping and testing into one final, polished, and fully working solution. This should solve your original problem to the extent which you chose in **Brainstorm**
7. **Communicate**
   - Communicate results, and make your findings and solution visible, so that in the future, other people who are doing similar things won't reinvent what you did, and instead are able to work off your progress.
